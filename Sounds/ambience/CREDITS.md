# The ambience's clips: credits

The world outside the lab, heard through the broadcast: the dead, the fighting, the people, the weather; and the sounds the story's events name (a helicopter, a phone, a typewriter), played when such an event is read.
Every clip was generated by the zombie-radio project with **Stable Audio 3 Small-SFX** (Stability AI, https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-3-small-sfx), from the prompts below, and chosen by ear.
